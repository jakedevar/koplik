### Q1. Units and rationale

**Units are correct and well-defined.** In `estimate_step`, `lambda_sum = Σ_{s∈window} Σ_k w_k I_{s-k}`. The weekly `w` is renormalised to sum to 1 (dropped mass 0.0045 for the default), so Λ_s is an SI-weighted average of earlier weekly counts, and ΣΛ over a window is the expected window incidence under R = 1 (equal to the weighted mean prior weekly count when τ = 1). It is a count, independent of grid step, but it scales with window length τ: a floor of 1.0 is a different stringency at τ = 2. The floor must be documented as "per estimation window of τ steps" and the default is tied to τ = 1.

**What the floor does and does not mean (derivable, not W40-specific):**
1. *Exact prior-weight identity.* With prior Gamma(a, scale b), the posterior mean is an exact convex combination of the prior mean and the data MLE: `(a+I)/(1/b+Λ) = [ (1/b)/(1/b+Λ) ]·(ab) + [ Λ/(1/b+Λ) ]·(I/Λ)`. The weight on the prior is `1/(1+bΛ)`, a function of Λ only, not of I. With b = 5: W40 (Λ = 0.392) has prior weight 0.338; Λ = 1.0 has 1/6 ≈ 0.167. So the 11-case gate (which bounds posterior CV through I) says nothing about prior weight, and Λ is the quantity that does. This is the best W40-independent justification for a Λ condition and the rationale text should use it. It also shows the floor is prior-dependent: if the prior scale changes the same Λ means a different prior weight. State the prior-weight equivalent next to the number.
2. *Why Λ, not I, governs uncertainty when offspring are overdispersed.* The Cori likelihood treats window cases as Poisson(R·Λ). If offspring counts are negative binomial with mean R and dispersion k (Lloyd-Smith et al. 2005, Nature 438:355–359), the variance of a ratio estimator is about `(R + R²/k)/n_infectors`, so CV² ≈ 1/I + 1/(k·Λ) (n_infectors ≈ Λ). The posterior CV of 1/√(a+I) captures only the first term. For k = 0.5, Λ = 1.0 the second term alone is CV² = 2. Therefore **Λ ≥ 1.0 is a necessary-condition screen and not an adequacy guarantee**: an estimate above the floor can still be very uncertain, and the status text, the doc comment and the UI must not claim "adequate" for estimates that pass. A CV-style target of 0.3 on this term would need Λ ≳ 11/k, so 1.0 is a deliberately weak floor, not a derived one.
3. *What it actually removes.* Given the existing gate I ≥ 11, any step newly withheld has Λ < 1, hence I/Λ > 11. Every newly withheld step already has an R̂ (MLE) above 11. No step with I/Λ ≤ 11 can be withheld. So the floor acts as a screen on "case count ≥ 11 times the preceding infector pool" onsets: outbreak starts, importation bursts, reporting batches, restarts after extinction — regimes where the renewal model assumption "window cases are generated by the observed previous cases" (Cori 2013, Methods/Discussion on importation and reporting assumptions) is least tenable. That is a defensible reading of "insufficient data". It is not a monotone ratio gate: I = 100 with Λ = 1.0 (ratio 100) passes, while I = 11, Λ = 0.99 (ratio 11.1) is withheld. Say so in the doc.

**Published basis.** To my knowledge the literature is silent on a numeric minimum for ΣΛ:
- Cori et al. 2013 (Am J Epidemiol 178:1505–1512): posterior Gamma(a+ΣI, 1/(1/b+ΣΛ)); cases-per-window / posterior CV discussion (the source of the "≈ 12 cases" idea); assumes constant R in window, complete observed infectors, stable reporting, imports handled via a separate imported series. No Λ threshold.
- EpiEstim `estimate_R.R`: `min_nb_cases_per_time_period <- ceiling(1/cv_posterior^2 - a_prior)` and a *warning* ("estimating R too early… desired posterior CV") on window incidence. It computes Λ and has an `imported` column. No Λ threshold anywhere in the package, to my knowledge.
- Thompson et al. 2019 (Epidemics 29:100356): improvements for imports/local split and SI uncertainty; recommends care early in outbreaks; no numeric Λ threshold known to me.
- Gostic et al. 2020 (PLoS Comput Biol 16:e1008409): emphasises reporting delays/right-truncation, estimating on onset incidence, reporting uncertainty; qualitative caution at low counts; no Λ rule.
- Nash et al. 2023 (PLoS Comput Biol): temporally aggregated incidence. **Relevant limitation**: applying a daily-style Cori model directly to weekly counts with an SI of ~1.7 weeks is a coarse approximation (Koplik additionally drops and renormalises same-week mass). The simulation must therefore generate at daily resolution and aggregate to weeks, or it is circular (R5).

The 1.0 is therefore a Koplik adequacy-policy choice. The proposal says so honestly. Keep that wording and add that the number is not derived from any of the sources above.

### Q2. Tuning risk and pre-registration order

- **The order is not clean, and the text must say so.** W40 (Λ = 0.392, I = 27) was observed *before* the rule and its number. Any floor in (0.392, ∞) withholds it; 1.0 is a round number above it. The pre-registration protects against tuning to the *sweep* and the *backtest*, not against W40 motivation. Record in the revised preregistration: "rule motivated by #1672 W40; the value 1.0 was fixed before any other published row's Λ was inspected; W40's removal is a consequence, not a criterion."
- Mitigations that make it acceptable: (a) the floor is justified by W40-independent arguments (prior weight, necessary-condition screen, overdispersion), (b) the value is frozen and tested by a simulation whose pass/fail criteria are fixed first (R3), (c) the outcome of failure is rejection, not re-picking, and (d) no result is judged by whether observed values disappear. The audit list (acceptance criterion 4) is report-only.
- **Not adequate as written:** acceptance criterion 2 lists scenario families but not the *success criteria*, replicate counts, truth definitions or seed scheme. Without them "documented coverage" cannot reject anything. R3 supplies them.
- Also fix the following: "boundary preserved at equality" is correct (published at ΣΛ = floor) but floating-point equality is fragile; test it with a constructed exactly-representable Λ (e.g. weights with a single lag equal to 1.0 in a hand-built `DiscreteSerialInterval`), not via a gamma SI.

### Q3. Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Posterior-CV criterion | Already used (I ≥ 11). Under the Poisson model CV = 1/√(a+I) regardless of Λ, so it cannot do the job; under overdispersion see CV² ≈ 1/I + 1/(kΛ). A CV bound on that needs a value of k that Koplik has no source for. Not adopted. |
| Prior-dominance criterion (`1/(1+bΛ) ≤ w_max`) | Mathematically equivalent to a Λ floor for a fixed prior; clearer rationale. Adopt it as the *explanatory* form (1.0 ⇔ w_max = 1/6 for b = 5). Do not make w_max the primary parameter: the contract and docs already speak of Λ. |
| Minimum known-history condition | Already enforced: `BeforeSeries::Unknown` makes any look-back reaching before the series `insufficient_data` (default). Not an alternative. |
| Incidence-ratio flag shown as a warning | Scientifically the best-targeted *diagnostic* (the W40 symptom is I/Λ = 69 versus measles R0 of roughly 12–18, Guerra et al. 2017, Lancet Infect Dis 17:e420), but a display flag needs a new contract field and a threshold that would be a biological ceiling, which #1677 excludes. Recommended as a separate, optional follow-up for the product owner; **not required to accept the floor and not a substitute.** Not filed here: it is a product choice, not a defect. |
| Cap, longer window, rescaling | Rejected by #1677 and by AGENTS.md rule 5; I concur. |

### Q4. Verdict and required revisions

**R3 — Simulation preregistration (commit a file under `thoughts/shared/` with this content, with its commit SHA recorded in the results file, BEFORE writing or running the simulator).**
- *Generator independent of the estimator:* daily individual-level branching process (not the weekly Poisson likelihood): infection times by gamma SI (mean 11.7 d, SD 3.0 d — the production value — plus one sensitivity run mean 14 d, SD 4 d), offspring Poisson or negative binomial with mean R; daily onsets aggregated to MMWR weeks; the estimator under test is `estimate_series` with `RenewalConfig::default()` and `BeforeSeries::Unknown` (and a `Zero` variant for the first scenario only).
- *Seeds:* portable RNG of the repo; replicate r of scenario s uses seed `s·1_000_003 + r`; 2000 replicates per scenario × parameter cell, 40 weeks each, first 6 weeks burn-in; list is committed.
- *Scenarios (parameters fixed now):* (1) steady: R ∈ {0.8, 1.0, 1.5, 3.0}, background importation 0.5 cases/week, dispersion k ∈ {∞, 1.0, 0.3} (stress values, not claimed measles estimates); (2) extinction/restart: R = 0.6 until chain extinction, then restart via importation at 0.2 clusters/week with local R = 1.5; (3) importation burst: R = 0.8 with 1/week background plus a single burst of 15 imported cases in one week; truth R = 0.8, estimator treats all as local (documented as the known misspecification); (4) reporting batches: underlying onset series as in (1) with R = 1.0, each week's reports are held with probability 0.1 and released with the next week's; truth is the underlying R; (5) ascertainment 0.3 by binomial thinning on (1) R = 1.0, k = 1.0.
- *Truth:* the generator's mean offspring number for infectors in the window's week (R_t of the cohort), not the Cori window estimand, and state this difference.
- *Metrics, all conditional on the existing gate I ≥ 11 and on Λ-bins [0,0.25), [0.25,0.5), [0.5,1), [1,2), [2,5), [5,∞):* number of steps; 95% and 50% credible-interval coverage of truth with Monte-Carlo standard error; median and 90th-percentile |log(R̂/R)|; fraction of steps with R̂/R > 3; fraction of all I ≥ 11 steps withheld by the floor. Report every cell, including empty cells.
- *Pass criteria (fixed now; the implementer may only tighten, not loosen, before running):* (S1) in at least three of scenarios 2–5 with ≥ 200 steps in the [0,1) bin, 95% CrI coverage in the [0,1) bin is ≤ 0.80 AND is at least 0.10 lower than coverage in the [1,2) bin of the same scenario; (S2) in scenario 1 with k = ∞ (correctly specified) coverage of the 95% CrI in the [1,2) bin is ≥ 0.93 (the floor does no harm where the model holds); (S3) report only, no criterion: the withheld fraction. Expectation, stated in advance so it is not read as post-hoc: under a correctly specified Poisson model a Bayesian estimate with a matching prior is roughly calibrated even at small Λ, so a benefit can only appear under the misspecified scenarios; if S1 fails the floor is unsupported and must not be implemented or retuned. If fewer than three scenarios reach 200 steps in the [0,1) bin the result is **inconclusive**: do not implement, report the counts.
